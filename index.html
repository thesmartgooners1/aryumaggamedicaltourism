Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about medical billing and RCM.

What is medical billing?

Medical billing translates services into claims, sends them to payers, and manages reimbursements and appeals.

Do you handle labs and imaging?

Yes, workflows for laboratory and radiology billing help manage payer-specific rules and documentation.

How fast can we see improvements?

Many providers see performance improvements within 60–90 days depending on volume and payer mix.
